# My Faithful DM scenarios
# Write one block per scenario. Separate blocks with a line containing only ---
# The two states are the only JSON. Copy them from the character's clean
# file and change only what the situation or the event changes.
# The Checks line is optional: leave it out and every field in the expected
# state gets checked. Common failure and Rules note are optional too.
# Also accept is optional: other answers that are just as correct by the rules,
# as JSON, e.g. {"conditions": [[]]}. It never changes the prompt.
# When a block is finished, run: python3 make_scenarios.py my_scenarios.txt
# That checks everything and writes my_scenarios.jsonl plus my_scenarios.csv.
# Lines starting with # like these are ignored anywhere in the file.

ID: S-01
Category: death_saves
Difficulty: medium
Character: Abigail
Starting state:
{"name": "Abigail", "class": "Wizard", "level": 3, "hp_current": 0, "hp_max": 20, "temp_hp": 0, "conditions": ["unconscious"], "death_saves": {"successes": 1, "failures": 2}, "dead": false}
Event:
Abigail rolls a death saving throw at the start of her turn. The roll result is given: 4. Nothing else happens.
Expected state:
{"name": "Abigail", "class": "Wizard", "level": 3, "hp_current": 0, "hp_max": 20, "temp_hp": 0, "conditions": ["unconscious"], "death_saves": {"successes": 1, "failures": 3}, "dead": true}
Also accept:
{"conditions": [[]]}
Common failure: Increments the failures to 3 but does not mark her dead, or adds two failures as if a low roll were a natural 1.
Rules note: A death saving throw of 1 to 9 is one failure. Three failures means the character dies. Only a natural 1 counts as two failures, and this roll was a 4. Also accepted: removing unconscious from conditions. The 2024 rules do not say whether a dead creature keeps the Unconscious condition, so both answers count.

ID: S-09
Category: concentration
Difficulty: medium
Character: Mark
Starting state:
{"name": "Mark", "class": "Rogue", "level": 3, "hp_current": 24, "hp_max": 24, "temp_hp": 0, "abilities": {"strength": 8, "dexterity": 16, "constitution": 15, "intelligence": 11, "wisdom": 13, "charisma": 12}, "concentration": "Faerie Fire"}
Event:
Mark takes 6 points of piercing damage. He makes a Constitution saving throw to keep his concentration. The d20 shows 8, before any modifiers.
Expected state:
{"name": "Mark", "class": "Rogue", "level": 3, "hp_current": 18, "hp_max": 24, "temp_hp": 0, "abilities": {"strength": 8, "dexterity": 16, "constitution": 15, "intelligence": 11, "wisdom": 13, "charisma": 12}, "concentration": "Faerie Fire"}
Common failure: Treats the bare d20 as the result, so 8 against DC 10 fails and concentration is dropped, or adds a proficiency bonus he does not have.
Rules note: Taking damage while concentrating forces a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher, so the DC is 10. Concentration holds and only the hit points change. Revised after review: the first version gave an ambiguous "roll of 8" and expected a failure, which is only right if 8 was already the total.

Difficulty: medium
Character: Mark

{"name": "Mark", "class": "Rogue", "level": 3, "hp_current": 20, "hp_max": 24, "temp_hp": 0, "inventory": [{"name": "Potion of Healing", "quantity": 2, "equipped": false}]}

As a Bonus Action, Mark drinks one Potion of Healing. The healing roll is given: 2d4 + 2 = 7.
Expected state:
{"name": "Mark", "class": "Rogue", "level": 3, "hp_current": 24, "hp_max": 24, "temp_hp": 0, "inventory": [{"name": "Potion of Healing", "quantity": 1, "equipped": false}]}

Mark's D&D Beyond sheet has no potions, so they are added for this scenario only, in the same list format the clean sheets use. Revised after review: the first version credited Fast Hands, which is a 2014 reading.
